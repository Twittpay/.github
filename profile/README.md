# TwittPay

**Accept bKash, Nagad, Rocket and Upay payments on your own website. Every payment is verified from the real transaction SMS.**

[Website](https://twittpay.com) · [Get your Brand Key](https://twittpay.com/user/brands)

</div>

## How it works

1. Create a Brand in your TwittPay dashboard and copy the **Brand Key**.
2. Install the addon for your platform from the list below.
3. Paste the Brand Key. Customers pay on the hosted TwittPay checkout, and your site verifies the payment on the server before marking the order paid.
